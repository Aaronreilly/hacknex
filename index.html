About VisionDetect

VisionDetect is an intelligent computer vision web platform engineered to identify, localize, and classify 80+ standard everyday objects, people, vehicles, and equipment with exceptional speed.

⚡ Zero-Server Latency

Runs client-side machine learning via TensorFlow.js WebGL GPU acceleration. Your media never leaves your device.

🎯 80+ Object Classes

Trained on the comprehensive COCO dataset recognizing people, bicycles, cars, animals, electronics, and accessories.

🎥 Video & Stream Ready

Supports static images, uploaded MP4/WebM videos, and direct webcam real-time tracking.

📊 Instant Analytics

Filter detections by confidence threshold, view per-class counts, and export annotated media or raw JSON.
